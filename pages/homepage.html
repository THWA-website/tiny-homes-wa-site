From contract signing to delivery, the typical timeline is 12–16 weeks. This includes engineering design (4–8 weeks), council approvals (2–6 weeks), manufacturing (7–9 weeks), and delivery/installation (1–3 days). We handle all permits and approvals on your behalf.

Our prices are fully installed — this includes the home, delivery to your site, installation, and connection to existing utilities. There are zero hidden costs. Your quote will itemise every component so you know exactly what you're paying for.
